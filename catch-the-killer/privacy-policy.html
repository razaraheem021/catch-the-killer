Catch the Killer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Catch the Killer (“the app”, “the game”) uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by [YOUR NAME] (“we”, “us”).

Summary

  • You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress and reminder settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • your case progress (clues found, scenes visited, difficulty, notes and accusations);
  • your daily reminder setting and chosen reminder time.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings › Apps › Catch the Killer › Storage › Clear storage). You can also start a new case at any time inside the game.

2. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in the game or in your phone’s settings at any time.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows banner, full-screen, rewarded, app-open and native ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • App interactions and diagnostics, such as ad views and taps, app launches, crash and performance information;
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • general device information, such as model, operating system version and language.

We do not see or store this information ourselves. Google processes it under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services and advertising
  • Google Privacy Policy

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, a consent message from Google asks whether ads may use your data for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time using Ad privacy choices on the game’s start screen.
  • Users in US states with privacy laws: where required, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through the same Ad privacy choices option.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

The version of the app without ads includes no advertising SDK and collects nothing in this section.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers or analytics for the app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location.

6. Children

The game features a murder-mystery story and is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and that data has been collected through ads, contact us and we will help; you can also reset the device’s Advertising ID.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To request deletion of advertising data held by Google, see Google’s data and privacy controls.

8. Security

The game runs entirely on your device and loads its content from inside the app. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
